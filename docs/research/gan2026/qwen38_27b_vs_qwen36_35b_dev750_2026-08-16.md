# Qwen 3.8 27B versus Qwen 3.6 35B on Gan `dev750`

Date: 2026-08-16  
Status: complete development readout  
Protocol: [Qwen 3.8 27B reserved-candidate measurement](../shared/qwen38_27b_candidate_protocol_2026-08-14.md)  
Split: Gan 2026 `validation` / `dev750` (inspectable). Not holdout.

## Finding

The similar hybrid finals hide a real model gap. On a no-call replay of both
matched v0.5 raw trees through HEAD `hybrid_full_stack`, Qwen 3.8 27B is
**+51 Purist at the model-selected label** (376 vs 325) and has **+169 exact
selected-evidence** rows (736 vs 567). After semantic repairs the same stack
**overtakes 3.8**: 666 vs 657 Purist, 684 vs 678 Pragmatic.

3.8 is the better extractor. The hybrid stack is a strong equalizer,
especially on 3.6's vaguer selected labels. Some 3.6-only Purist wins are
gold-convention wins: dated-sequence / diary floors overwrite a bare
`seizure free` into the calendar count, while 3.8's more precise “seizure
free since DATE” quote stays seizure-free.

This is not a Decision 0051 roster swap and does not rewrite Qwen 3.6:35B
primary fills.

## Exact evidence

Exact means the selected evidence string is a case-sensitive contiguous
substring of the note. It is not clinical validity.

| Model | Exact | Ellipsis join | Other non-exact |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Qwen 3.6 35B | 567 | 140 | 43 |
| Qwen 3.8 27B | **736** | 11 | 3 |

3.6 still synthesizes multi-span citations with `...`. 3.8 almost always
copies one contiguous span. That is a genuine model improvement and the
clearest operational sign that 3.8 is better, even when the hybrid score is
not.

Repair **counts** are almost the same: 528 vs 526 rows with at least one

| Prediction-bearing harm | 6 |
| No repair | 224 |

On the 528 3.6-repaired rows, **193** have 3.8 already **exact and
Purist-correct at the model boundary**.

Those 193 split as:

- **132 same-category renders.** 3.6 wrote a slightly illegal form
  (`seizure free`, `1 per 2 weeks`, `daily`) and rules canonicalized it.
  3.8 already wrote a scorer-legal label (`seizure free for 12 months`,
  `1 per day`). 3.8 did not miss a rescue; it did not need one.
- **56 prediction-bearing rescues** that 3.8 did not need because its raw
  selection was already in the right Purist bucket. Typical 3.6 hops:
  `daily → 1 per day`, `several per week → multiple per week`,
  `multiple per day → 4 per day`, `nightly → 1 per day`.
- **5** other prediction-bearing hops.

Of 3.6's **313** prediction-bearing rescues, 3.8 is also finally correct on
**278**, and already correct at the model boundary on **57**. 3.8's evidence
on those 313 rescue rows is exact on 306; 3.6's is exact on 240 (59 ellipsis).

Most of 3.6's huge raw→final jump is shared machinery applied to a sloppier
selected label. 3.8 often starts closer to the Gan surface.

## Why 3.8 can lose after being more precise

The leftover is the interesting part: rules that fire on a sloppy
`seizure free` or `multiple per week` do not fire the same way on a precise
dated seizure-free quote or a fragment-level cluster quote.

## What this tells us

1. **3.8 is the better extractor** at the model boundary: +51 Purist, +169
   exact evidence, almost no ellipsis synthesis, and 283 disagreements that
   rules have to paper over.
2. **The hybrid stack is a strong equalizer.** Selected-evidence rewrite,
   cluster completion, and dated-sequence overwrite do more work for 3.6
   because 3.6 more often emits a vague or illegal label those rules were
   written to catch.
3. **3.8 does not fail to benefit from most of those rules.** On 278/313 of
   3.6's rescues, 3.8 is also finally correct. On 193 repaired-3.6 rows it
   never needed the hop.
4. **Some 3.6 “wins” are gold-convention wins.** If gold wants the dated
   event-list rate and the note also has a current seizure-free sentence,
   3.8's more faithful quote of that sentence loses. That is a known
   hybrid/gold tension, not a 3.6 comprehension advantage.
5. That also fits holdout aggregate shape: a more precise current-status
   model loses on the SF-versus-dated-count and unknown-versus-committed
   slices.

Do not retune `hybrid_full_stack` from these holdout-shaped leftovers. A
holdout defect starts a new development candidate; it does not license
holdout repair. Any follow-up that wants to credit current-status quotes
over dated event lists needs a predeclared gold-free development predicate.
