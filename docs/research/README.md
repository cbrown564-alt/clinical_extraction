and [navigation](../NAVIGATION.md) for selected evidence owners. A protocol or

results and labels do not transfer to the new longitudinal benchmark.

Its machine-readable evidence retains original paths and bytes. Dissertation prose
lives beside its manuscript; historical reports are evidence, not current queues.
