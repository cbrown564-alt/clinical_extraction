The risks are now sharper, not hidden.

The project can claim the dev140 headline target while still naming which gains are portable clinical behavior and which are benchmark-surface repairs.

A portable clinical-extraction principle for a completed-test target.

Mostly portable parser behavior, with residual risk around titration plans and annotation oddities.

The main caveat is SeizureFrequency fidelity.

SF headline F1 is above target, but active-rate fidelity remains 0.5969. The improvement is best understood as a type/state headline gain, not solved quantitative-rate extraction.

What this changes.

v08 is a research milestone because it clarifies the mechanism of improvement, not because it erases the need for a locked evaluation.

Research implication

Assembly beats monolithic prompting here.

Focused producers mattered, but the stable lift came from auditable family-specific reconciliation.

Reporting implication

Headline and companion surfaces must travel together.

The page should never let 0.9152 stand alone without benchmark and fidelity context.
