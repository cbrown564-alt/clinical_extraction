How one letter becomes a fact inventory

The model proposes all four families in one structured call. Deterministic stages then project attributes, apply selected family rules, require evidence, and assemble the final set.

  1. Interpret the whole letter. Produce candidate events and source spans.
  2. Project and check attributes. Remove candidates that do not satisfy the family contract.
  3. Apply family rules. Diagnosis and seizure-frequency stages make the largest helpful changes; Prescription has a measured harm surface.
  4. Return a coherent set. Score the final facts under the four-family project measure.

Supported

  • A three-method comparison for living Grok on the cited ExECT methods.
  • Six-model aggregate holdout scores and family ranges.
  • Development evidence of family-specific rescues, harms, and no-ops.

Not supported

  • Reproduction of the published ExECT benchmark.
  • Clinical validation or deployment readiness.
  • A claim that every deterministic family transform improves F1.
