Reliability is assessed, not pooled.

Gan and ExECT answer the same eight reliability questions with different measures. Every question has retained evidence, but much of it is partial and none produces a shared reliability score. Gold agreement is how this paper scores a designed form; it is not clinical truth.

Reading ruleCompare what was measured, how strong the evidence is, and what remains missing. Do not compare the task numbers as if they measured the same thing.

Clinically meaningful correctness

Benchmark agreement is a measured proxy. It is not absolute clinical truth.

Evidence-grounded review

Source presence, semantic support, and reviewer usefulness are separate questions.

Visible uncertainty and failure

Unknown, competing evidence, corrections, and operational failures must remain inspectable.

What the paper can say

  • The same eight questions were applied to both tasks.
  • Evidence states and task-specific limits are explicit.
  • Selected decisions, evidence, corrections, and failures can be replayed and inspected.

What remains unsupported

  • One shared reliability metric or composite score.
  • Independent clinical validation or deployment reliability.
  • Demographic fairness, performance transfer, or matched efficiency.
