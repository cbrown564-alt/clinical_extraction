## Primary question

What paper-facing wording may the 2026-08-06 hybrid mechanism ladder support
**beside** existing claims C16 (Gan final ruleset scores) and C17 / Decision
0046 (ExECT primary three-method fills), and what wording must the paper still
avoid?

## Why it matters

The mechanism ladder now has stronger development attribution language than the
six-model comparison’s aggregate reading. Without an explicit claim-boundary
package, manuscript prose can either under-use the mechanism evidence or
accidentally rewrite C16 / Decision 0046 score ownership.

## Scope

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Score fills | **Unchanged** — C16 and Decision 0046 / C17 numbers stay as recorded |
| Evidence | Retained 2026-08-06 no-call ladder + residual studies already shipped |
| Calls | none |
| Holdout | Only previously published aggregate-only family lenses; no new sealed-row work |
| Canon | May propose companion claims; must not weaken C16/C17 limits |

## Method

1. Restate what C16 and Decision 0046 / C17 already own.
2. Map ladder findings to allowed companion wording with strength labels.
3. List forbidden upgrades that would overclaim residuals, LOO, or holdout.
4. Emit a machine-readable claim ledger and paste-ready manuscript paragraphs.
5. Recommend at most two bounded companion rows for
   `docs/canon/10_paper_provenance.md` if they are strictly weaker than a
   score rewrite.

## Stop rule

Answer when: (a) C16 and 0046 fills are explicitly preserved; (b) allowed vs
forbidden wording is listed with evidence owners; (c) companion-claim
recommendation is accept / reject / defer with strength.

## Claim boundary

Packaging and claim-strength guidance only. Not a Decision 0046 rewrite. Not a
C16 score revision. Not clinical validation. Not authorization to change
default repairs or lenses.
