Interpretation

The finding is portability with caveats, not raw parity.

Qwen is extracting useful clinical material, but the final score often depends on deterministic layers that add, drop, rewrite, normalize, or canonicalize the answer. Hiding that repair makes the model comparison look cleaner than it is.
