# Owner batch decisions: saved R8 development replay

This is an additive version of the [v0.8.2 all-letter audit](../../seizure_finding_annotation_v0_8_2/dev750_r8_saved/README.md) for Gan 2026 **synthetic dev750**. The [annotation guide](../../../../../docs/research/gan2026/seizure_finding_annotation_v08.md) owns the rules. All 750 development letters and the same 714 usable saved R8 projections were rescored. No model was called; the 36 invalid saved inventories remain invalid. Earlier references, scores, and the locked split were not changed.

The producer is `scripts/benchmarks/revise_findings_v083.py`. It applies **81 source-backed edits across 63 letters** to the frozen v0.8.2 reference. The local `runs/seizure_finding_annotation_v0_8_3/dev750_r8_saved/claim_adjudications.jsonl` records each source ID/hash, quotation, reason, and before/after finding. Fifty-three edits make a recurring cluster counted unit explicit in the event label; the remaining 28 implement the diary, summary, and uncertain-event decisions. The scorer remains `finding_v082_v1`.

The lower whole-finding score is **a consequence of changing labels and inventory units against fixed R8 predictions**, not evidence of a worse model run. Fifteen of the 23 lost matches occur on letters with only cluster-unit relabelling. A recurring pattern previously labelled `seizures` now names `clusters` or `clusters of [subtype]`; a saved prediction that still says `seizures` loses the whole-finding match despite possibly getting the numeric cadence right. The separate v0.8.1 component diagnostic remains available for studying subtype and measurement independently; it has not been recomputed against v0.8.3.

## Applied decisions

1. **Overall total where arithmetic is clear.** [5995](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=5995) has two generalised convulsions and three individual absence events inside one cluster: five individual seizures in January–September. It retains the convulsion count and the absence-cluster measurement. The zero months and standalone “infrequent” description are context. [15982](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=15982) adds four nocturnal and five daytime events to nine overall. [16041](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=16041) combines five October daytime and two December awake events to seven daytime, plus two nocturnal events, making nine overall. [15986](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=15986) already has an overall count of eleven; its progression to bilateral tonic–clonic seizure is a subset and is not added again. A zero nocturnal subtype in [15992](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=15992) does not create an extra overall finding identical to the seven daytime events.
2. **One additional broad absence summary.** [9190](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=9190) retains the February focal impaired-awareness/convulsion absence, April aura absence, and broad no-clinical-seizures summary. Its vague “occasional auras” background, supervisor corroboration of the February absence, and later broad restatement are context. The specific absence now names both event types.
3. **Recurring cluster pattern.** [15442](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=15442) labels recurring days with two tonic seizures after seizure-free days as `clusters of tonic seizures`, although that sentence does not say “cluster.” [8969](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=8969) already labels weekly clusters correctly. Every `cluster` measurement with a recurring rate and the source-checked recurring size patterns now names the cluster unit. Explicit single-cluster cases remain available for the next review.
4. **Clinical link for uncertain symptoms.** [4694](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=4694) retains observed behavioural pauses and EEG seizures while dropping non-specific nocturnal restlessness. [7195](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=7195) drops an isolated “possible brief event.” [15783](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=15783) drops unwitnessed nocturnal events inferred only from bed disarray and morning confusion. [8144](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=8144) retains déjà vu sensations because the clinician later calls them the patient's auras.

## Next group of examples for policy review

- **One-off grouping versus a cluster pattern.** [16772](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=16772) calls a single seizure a “run of one” and currently uses `cluster`; [16645](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=16645) gives one cluster of three; [16757](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=16757) gives a single run of six in half an hour. Should isolated groups without a recurring pattern use an individual-seizure count, a cluster count/size, or both? The answer affects many one-off source templates.
- **Affected days and cluster size.** [11109](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=11109), [11118](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=11118), and [11131](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=11131) say two cluster days in a month, each typically containing a stated number of seizures. Gold currently splits affected-day count from seizures per cluster; R8 combines them in one cluster finding. Should those be one structured finding or two scored measurements?
- **Clinically suspected episodes.** [3528](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=3528) describes loss-of-concentration spells “suggestive of brief absences” and later calls them a relapse in smaller events; [6738](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=6738) documents recurrent blank moments with confusion as spells of concern, under investigation; [8144](http://localhost:3000/workbench?dataset=ganR8&version=v083&letter=8144) explicitly identifies brief sensations as prior auras. Where exactly does clinical concern become enough identification to score the rate?

The three groups above are retained for owner review. Their present labels have not been silently rewritten to force a score change.
