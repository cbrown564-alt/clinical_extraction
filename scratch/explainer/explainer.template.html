The supervisor layer for the supervisor

Every stage, who owns it, and what it may change

Each method is declared in a machine-readable stage manifest; this section is rendered from those manifests, not written by hand. Two vocabularies carry the whole system: the owner (model, deterministic, scorer) and the effect class (what a stage is allowed to change).

The machine room for the supervisor

Five commands, token by token

Two commands turn your own notes into answers. One reproduces research runs on permitted splits. One serves saved runs to the frontend. One regenerates the explanation layer. The CLI map is hand-authored from the argument parsers and labelled as such; the stage wiring beneath it is derived from the manifests.

Where this page comes from

Boundaries: the teaching letters are synthetic and the model outputs are labelled fixtures — this page is teaching material, not clinical benchmark evidence. Locked test rows are never read here.
