The original task asks for one selected current seizure-frequency label. Our richer output also retains measurements, time, uncertainty and quotations. These extra findings may help a later researcher ask a different question. But asking for them creates more things that can be wrong.

01 / Meaning

Keep what the sentence says before deciding what number to report.

The measurement review argues that a number needs a subject, a counting unit and a time role. These relationships matter more than the number of fields in a schema. Review 1

Three seizures since the previous appointment.
Keep a count and its unresolved interval

The letter gives three events. It does not give the time between appointments.

Do not turn it into three seizures per month.

Three clusters per month, with four seizures in each.
Keep both counting levels

Three counts clusters; four counts seizures within a cluster. Under an exact reading, their product describes twelve seizures per month within that process.

Do not discard the two source measurements after deriving a total.

Four seizures weekly, lasting ninety seconds each.
Keep the two time roles apart

A week is the rate denominator. Ninety seconds is the duration of an event.

A time expression is not automatically a denominator.

No convulsions since surgery, but auras remain daily.
Keep absence attached to its event type

The statement about convulsions and the daily aura rate have different scopes.

Do not report that all seizures have stopped.

The last seizure was six months ago.
Keep recency as recency

This gives a last-event time. It does not describe a recurring event every six months.

Do not treat it as one-sixth of a seizure per month in the source record.

Already agreed

V2 preserves typed measurements, event scope, bounds, approximation, conditions, time wording and linked evidence. Unknown and no-reference states remain distinct. Generic confidence and negation fields were deliberately removed.

Recommended · before the next freeze

Check three gaps with fictional examples: cluster counts over an interval, seizure-day counts, and supporting context outside the main quote. If the schema cannot express them honestly, make the smallest correction. Do not redesign the whole ontology or change a running comparison.

Why “cluster” deserves another look

“Three clusters per month” is a rate. “Three clusters since review” is a count over an interval. The counted thing is a cluster in both cases. Making counting unit independent of count versus rate can express both. This is a targeted design recommendation, not evidence that the current model has made this error.

Likewise, “two seizure days” does not mean two seizures. The schema should preserve the named unit or explicitly retain the phrase without pretending to know an individual-event count.

Illustrative counts only. A simple system asked for one answer has not failed its own task by omitting an unrequested inventory. The completeness comparison here applies to the stated richer task.

Can assess without a full inventory

Is this emitted finding supported?

A reviewer can read the full note and judge the finding’s quantity, type, unit, time and uncertainty. The source note supplies the evidence.

Needs a coverage reference

What did the model leave out?

Someone must identify the required source findings independently. Checking only the model’s output cannot discover a finding that never appears there.

The highest-value addition is a small support audit. Full inventory annotation is a larger, separate commitment.

Define the judgement

Judge a whole measurement with its decisive qualifiers. A correct number attached to the wrong seizure type fails. Keep “unresolved” separate from “supported”.

Sample letters, then review their findings

Choose permitted letters without selecting only interesting errors. Inspect every emitted finding against the full note. Keep deliberately difficult examples separate from estimates of ordinary performance.

Report the added errors

Show supported, unsupported and unresolved findings; records containing any unsupported finding; and review disagreements. Report empty or unusable outputs separately.

Keep the claim within the sample

A development audit is development evidence. Patient review needs explicit permission. Neither gives inventory recall without independent source-first annotation.

Recommended · scope decision needed

Prioritise this audit over adding another model or clinical family. The current protocol does not require it, so adopt it explicitly if feasible. If it cannot be done, keep the narrower study and state that the extra findings have not been evaluated for semantic correctness.

03 / Evidence

A real quote can support
a wrong interpretation.

The attribution review gives us a precise positive claim: the record makes source support inspectable. It does not reveal the model’s internal reasoning. Review 3

Fictional attribution error
Quote: “No convulsions for two years.”
Finding: “No seizures for two years.”

The quotation is exact. The finding broadens its meaning. A successful substring check would miss the error.

1 · Occurrence

Does the quoted text exist in the note?

Check: exact text matching.

2 · Support

Does the note support the whole finding?

Check: semantic review, including context.

3 · Consistency

Does the answer fit the recorded findings and stated task rules?

Check: a defined policy or adjudication.

4 · Causal dependence

Did those findings actually influence the model’s answer?

Check: a separate intervention study.

These are separate questions, not automatic steps up a ladder. A record can pass the first three and still omit an important finding. It can also agree with a selection policy without showing that the model used that policy.

Use in the paper

“Source-linked extraction record”

Say exactly which properties were checked. If only quotation occurrence was measured, call it quotation occurrence.

Avoid without evidence

“Faithful explanation”

Do not infer causal reasoning, complete evidence recovery or better human decisions from a plausible record.

Already agreed

The model declares the answer in the same call. Parsing and format handling must not choose another event or repair clinical meaning.

Recommended

Use answer–finding consistency as a diagnostic where the policy is sufficiently specified. Save an authoritative deterministic selector for a separate hybrid method. That would create a real downstream dependency, but it would change this paper’s intervention.

04 / Performance

More output changes the work.
Measure the cost before naming the cause.

The output-requirements review identifies four different changes that are often bundled together. Research showing an effect of one does not establish the effect of all four in our task. Review 4

Scope

One selected answer → all scoped measurements.

Representation

The same information in nested objects or linked fields.

Generation order

Findings before the answer, or the answer before findings.

Decoding constraints

Asking for JSON versus enforcing its syntax or schema.

Interpretation

The gap is real for this endpoint. Its cause is not established.

Strict scoring counts an unusable rich record as wrong even if its declared label could be read independently. The 72 schema failures therefore matter to interpretation.

They do not establish that all the loss is formatting, or that those answers were correct. No truncations weakens a truncation explanation; it does not establish inventory completeness.

Previously exposed synthetic development data. These are not patient results or holdout generalisation.

Already in the newer development comparison

Thinking-enabled r4 simple and r5 rich distinguish independent answer checks from full-record validity and strict agreement. The r6 original comparator retains its historical parser, so its validity counts have different meanings. No completed results from that running comparison are asserted here.

Recommended

Bring these distinctions into the results table alongside tokens, latency and failures. Preserve older frozen endpoints. Describe any newly added retrospective diagnostic as retrospective. Do not present a changed measure as recovery of the original score.

05 / Choices

Take the useful distinctions.
Keep the larger studies separate.

The reviews support a more precise paper. They do not require us to adopt every proposed architecture, metric or annotation programme.

Current decisions

Keep

One-call frequency extraction. Separate source measurements and the declared answer. Matched rich/simple conditions. Native Purist and Pragmatic scoring. Explicit first-attempt failures.

Recommended for this paper

Strengthen

Separate answer and record outcomes. Test the few remaining representation gaps. Add a bounded support audit if feasible. Describe source support precisely.

Follow-up exploration

Expand later

Full inventory references. Factor-by-factor mechanism experiments. Deterministic derivation. Cross-note timelines. Interoperability. Human review and causal-faithfulness studies.

A label-only answer can be adequate for the original task. Our case for richer output rests on preserving information for additional questions—and on measuring how well we do that.

First follow-up: a dual-reference benchmark

Keep the selected answer reference and add an independently annotated seizure-measurement inventory on a bounded sample. Then measure answer agreement, finding precision and inventory recall on the same letters.

Second follow-up: test one suspected cause

If development errors point to generation order, change only order. If they point to representation, compare two lossless encodings. A focused experiment is more informative than an uncontrolled collection of variants.

Where the planned multi-family extension fits

The repository already plans a separate patient extension across several clinical families. This discussion recommends prioritising evidence quality before broadening it; it does not cancel that plan. Its fields, permissions and reference availability remain separate decisions. The Gan frequency label cannot score additional diagnosis, prescription or investigation findings.

Proposed paper framing

We test whether one model call can produce an inspectable record of seizure-frequency measurements alongside the established task answer. We report answer agreement, output reliability and cost separately. Claims about finding support depend on the audit actually performed; completeness and causal explanation remain separate questions.
