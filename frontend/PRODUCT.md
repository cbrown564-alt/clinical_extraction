# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The researcher presents the submitted extraction method to the supervisor and assessor. The workbench supports deeper inspection during questions.

## Product Purpose

Make a clinic letter, the extracted evidence, and the resulting decision easy to follow in a short demonstration. Preserve source links and distinguish saved output from fresh execution.

## Positioning

One shared extraction record makes the two decision executors inspectable and comparable.

## Design Principles

Prove one complete letter-to-decision interaction before extending it. Keep the document beside its evidence. Reveal detail as the explanation progresses. Use the submitted extract/decide terminology. Show failures with the same care as successful examples.

## Brand Personality

Clear, restrained, precise. The existing workbench supplies the visual starting point.

## Anti-references

Visually complex pipeline diagrams and dense research controls that require explanation before the research itself can be understood.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable presentation text, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus and a reduced-motion alternative. Evidence connections must remain understandable without colour.
