Epilepsy clinic letter
Consultation: 12 February 2025
Patient: Kevin O'Connor, aged 45

Dear colleague,

Kevin attended clinic today. He has an established diagnosis of focal motor epilepsy.

His seizures typically occur in severe explosive clusters rather than isolated events. On 5 and 6 February 2025, over a 48-hour period, he suffered an acute cluster of 5 focal motor seizures with left facial and arm clonic activity. He had no seizures between that cluster and today's review. He reports no convulsions. He continues carbamazepine 400 mg twice daily.

No changes were made to his regimen. No EEG requested. Review in four months.

A second pattern consists of brief bilateral arm shaking without facial involvement. These shaking episodes and his focal motor clusters have both recurred since November 2024, including throughout January and early February. I currently regard both as epileptic. These are the only two patterns reported since November; they are distinct events.
