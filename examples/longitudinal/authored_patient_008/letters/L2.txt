Epilepsy clinic letter
Consultation: 20 February 2025
Patient: Hannah Clarke

Dear colleague,

Hannah re-attended clinic today after an eighteen-month interval, having missed scheduled interim appointments. Her diagnosis remains focal epilepsy.
