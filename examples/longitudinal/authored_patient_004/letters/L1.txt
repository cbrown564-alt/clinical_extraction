Epilepsy clinic letter
Consultation: 20 January 2025
Patient: Danielle Kim, aged 51

Dear colleague,

Danielle attended our epilepsy review clinic. She has a longstanding history of temporal lobe epilepsy.

Her typical pattern has been complex partial seizures occurring approximately twice a month, characterized by behavioral arrest, lip smacking and post-ictal amnesia. Her latest complex partial seizure occurred on 5 January 2025. In the distant past she had grand mal convulsions, but she has suffered no grand mal attacks since December 2021. She continues carbamazepine 200 mg three times daily.

We agreed to keep her carbamazepine unchanged. No EEG was requested. Review in four months.
